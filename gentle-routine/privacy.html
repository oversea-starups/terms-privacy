Privacy Policy

For Gentle Routine. Last updated: 2026-11-14.

Gentle Routine is provided by Beijing baqijiuyi Technology Co., Ltd. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information kept on your device

You may enter personal plan steps, timing, notes, sensations, interruption reasons, and session records. The app stores these records locally on your iPhone. Language preferences and optional reminder settings are also stored locally.

No developer collection or tracking

Gentle Routine has no account system, remote backend, analytics, advertising, tracking, or network upload. We do not receive the records you enter. The app does not use HealthKit or collect device health data.

Notifications

If you enable the optional reminder, iOS schedules a local notification on your device. The app uses neutral reminder text. iOS controls permission, Focus behavior, delivery, and lock-screen presentation.

Exports and sharing

You can create a PDF summary or raw JSON archive and choose a recipient through the iOS share sheet. Sharing is your choice. Copies saved to Files, sent to another person, or retained by another app are outside our control.

Deletion and retention

You can delete individual sessions, demo data, or all app data in Settings. Temporary export files created by the app are cleared when the share sheet closes and during deletion. Uninstalling the app or losing the device can remove local records. The app excludes its records from ordinary device backups, so export before switching devices.

Children

Gentle Routine is intended for adults who already have a professionally assessed personal arrangement. It is not directed to children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy by posting a new version on this page. Questions about privacy can be sent to vokie123@gmail.com.

Product boundary: Gentle Routine records a user-owned arrangement. It does not diagnose, prescribe treatment, assess outcomes, or recommend progression.

隐私政策

适用于 Gentle Routine。最后更新：2026-11-14。

Gentle Routine 由北京捌柒玖壹科技有限公司提供。本政策说明应用如何处理信息。

保存在设备上的信息

你可以录入个人计划步骤、时长、备注、感受、中断原因和练习记录。应用会将这些内容保存在你的 iPhone 本地。语言偏好和可选提醒设置也保存在本地。

不收集、不追踪

Gentle Routine 没有账号系统、远程后台、分析统计、广告、追踪或网络上传。我们不会收到你录入的记录。应用不使用 HealthKit，也不收集设备健康数据。

通知

如果你开启可选提醒，iOS 会在设备上安排本地通知。应用使用中性提醒文字。权限、专注模式、送达和锁屏显示由 iOS 控制。

导出和分享

你可以创建 PDF 摘要或原始 JSON 归档，并通过 iOS 分享面板自行选择接收对象。分享由你决定。保存到“文件”、发送给他人或被其他应用保留的副本不受本应用控制。

删除和保留

你可以在设置中删除单条记录、演示数据或全部应用数据。应用创建的临时导出文件会在分享面板关闭和删除数据时清理。删除应用或设备丢失可能导致本地记录丢失。应用记录排除在普通设备备份之外，换机前请先导出。

儿童

Gentle Routine 面向已经拥有专业评估个人安排的成人，不面向儿童。
