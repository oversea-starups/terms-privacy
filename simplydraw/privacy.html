Privacy Policy

For Learn to Draw on Paper. Last updated: 2026-07-29.

Beijing baqijiuyi Technology Co., Ltd operates Learn to Draw on Paper. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, disclosed, and protected when you use the app, website, or related services.

Information We Collect

  • Local guest data: learner profiles, preferences, lesson progress, and saved paper-artwork photos remain in app-managed storage on the device. Local guest data is not uploaded or synced to our server.
  • Signed-in account data: an Apple-linked user identifier; name and email when Apple supplies them; learner profiles and preferences; lesson progress and product interaction.
  • User content: paper-artwork photos and related content that a signed-in user chooses to upload.
  • Purchases: purchase history and subscription entitlement state received from Apple. We do not receive or store payment-card details.
  • Optional practice feedback: inputs and results requested by an entitled user. Our backend performs deterministic pixel, edge, contour, and contrast comparison. It does not use an external AI provider and does not generate or complete artwork.
  • Support and operations: information you include in a support request and limited technical information needed to secure and operate the service.

We collect information only as needed to provide, maintain, secure, and improve Learn to Draw on Paper.

Camera And Photo Library

Camera access is requested only when you choose to photograph a paper artwork. Photo-library access is requested only when you choose an existing image. Simply opening the app does not grant access or upload a photo.

How We Use Information

  • Provide and operate the service.
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot issues.
  • Respond to support, privacy, or legal requests.
  • Improve features, performance, and user experience.
  • Process purchases or subscriptions when applicable.

Third-Party Services

Learn to Draw on Paper uses Apple for Sign in with Apple, App Store distribution, and StoreKit purchases. Service infrastructure providers may process data only to host the API, database, and private signed-account storage. Version 1.0 has no advertising, attribution, external analytics, or crash-reporting SDK.

  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, App Store distribution, StoreKit purchases, subscription management, and refund mechanisms.
  • Vercel: API hosting and service delivery.
  • Neon: PostgreSQL database hosting for signed-account data.
  • Cloudflare R2: private object storage for artwork uploaded by signed-in users.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers, app stores, legal authorities when required, or as part of a business transfer, only as permitted by applicable law.

Data Retention

Local guest data remains on the device until you delete an item, clear local data, or remove the app in a way that deletes its data. Signed-account data is retained while needed to provide the service and then deleted, de-identified, or retained only where required for security, disputes, refunds, tax, or other legal obligations. We do not promise a fixed deletion period that the production system cannot verify.

Your Choices And Rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or other privacy assistance by contacting us. Local guests can clear app-managed profiles, progress, and artwork in the app. Signed-in users can request account deletion in Profile > Settings; deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription. Some rights depend on your location and applicable law.

Children

Learn to Draw on Paper is a general-audience drawing-learning service and is not designated Made for Kids. Its presentation may be welcoming to younger beginners, but it does not provide an independent parental-consent workflow. A minor should use the service with a parent or guardian when required by applicable law. If you believe a child provided personal information without appropriate authorization, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

International Users

Your information may be processed in countries other than where you live. We use reasonable safeguards where required by applicable law.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new last-updated date.
