Privacy Policy

For World Panorama Map. Last updated: 2026-08-05.

北京捌柒玖壹科技有限公司 operates World Panorama Map. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, disclosed, and protected when you use the app, website, or related services.

Information We Collect

  • Place names, administrative regions, countries, and coordinates that you deliberately select for AI generation.
  • A random installation identifier, stored by our service only as a one-way hash with quota counts.
  • Apple-signed purchase transaction data, processed transiently to verify Pro access and not retained by our service.
  • The server-authored prompt and generated image, processed transiently for delivery. A generated image is stored only in the app's on-device gallery when you choose to save it.
  • Limited operational data such as request time, response status, network information used for abuse prevention, and technical errors. Place prompts, transaction tokens, and generated images are excluded from our application logs.

We collect information only as needed to provide, maintain, secure, and improve World Panorama Map.

How We Use Information

  • Provide and operate the service.
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot issues.
  • Respond to support, privacy, or legal requests.
  • Improve features, performance, and user experience.
  • Process purchases or subscriptions when applicable.

Third-Party Services

World Panorama Map relies on the services below for maps, purchases, image generation, cloud hosting, quota storage, and limited operational diagnostics. This release does not use advertising services.

  • Apple MapKit and Look Around
  • Apple App Store and StoreKit
  • Cloud hosting, quota storage, and image-generation service providers acting on our instructions

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers, app stores, legal authorities when required, or as part of a business transfer, only as permitted by applicable law.

Data Retention

Quota records containing the hashed installation identifier are automatically deleted after 90 days. Selected places, Apple transaction tokens, prompts, and generated images are not retained by our service after the request completes. Images you save remain on your device until you delete them or the app. Limited security or diagnostic records are retained only as long as reasonably needed for abuse prevention and troubleshooting, unless law requires longer retention.

Your Choices And Rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or other privacy assistance by contacting us. Some rights depend on your location and applicable law.

Children

World Panorama Map is not directed to children under 13 unless explicitly stated in the service. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

International Users

Your information may be processed in countries other than where you live. We use reasonable safeguards where required by applicable law.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new last-updated date.
