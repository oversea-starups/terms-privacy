Privacy Policy

For Flow Sort Lab: Cargo Puzzle. Last updated: 2026-07-30.

Beijing baqijiuyi Technology Co., Ltd operates Flow Sort Lab: Cargo Puzzle. This policy describes version 1.0 of the iOS app.

Information and local storage

Version 1.0 does not send personal information, gameplay history, advertising identifiers, contacts, location, photos, or audio to a developer-operated server. Game progress, settings, virtual-currency balances, and StoreKit transaction identifiers used to prevent duplicate delivery are stored locally on the device.

Purchases

Optional consumable purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. Apple may process account, payment, tax, fraud-prevention, and transaction information under its own terms and privacy policy. The app receives product identifiers and transaction status needed to deliver a purchase; the publisher does not receive full payment-card details.

Advertising and analytics

Version 1.0 contains no advertising SDK and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK. It does not use tracking for targeted advertising.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on the device until the app's data is removed or the app is uninstalled, subject to iOS backup and restore behavior controlled by the user and Apple. Because version 1.0 has no developer account system or backend, the publisher cannot identify or remotely delete a particular device's local save.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for a general audience and is not offered in the Kids Category. Version 1.0 does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Support communications

If you email support, the publisher receives the email address, message, and any technical information you choose to provide. This information is used to respond to the request, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations, and is retained only as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new last-updated date.
