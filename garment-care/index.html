Garment Care Support

Last updated: 2026-11-13

Garment Care is a free offline organizer for people who rotate several compression garments. It records the statuses and dates you choose; it does not diagnose, treat, measure, or recommend medical care.

How to use the app

  • Add a garment and record its name, optional photo, side, area, and your own instructions.
  • Move its status through Wearing, To wash, Drying, and Ready when you confirm the next step yourself.
  • Use Packing for a dated checklist and Care for one-time dates you choose.
  • Open Settings to change language, add labeled sample data, export JSON, or delete local data.

Data and support

Records stay on the iPhone unless you explicitly choose Export or Share in iOS. There is no account, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, tracking, payment, or backend service in this build.

压力衣轮换助手支持

最后更新：2026-11-13

压力衣轮换助手是一款免费、离线的记录工具，帮助需要轮换多件压力衣的人整理状态和日期。所有状态和日期都由你记录；App 不诊断、治疗、测量，也不推荐护理方案。

如何使用

  • 添加衣物，记录名称、可选照片、左右侧、部位和你自己的说明。
  • 按自己的实际流程更新“穿着、待洗、晾干中、可用”，确认下一步后再手动更新。
  • 在“出行清单”建立带日期的清单，在“护理事项”记录自己设定的一次性日期。
  • 在“设置”切换语言、添加带标签的示例、导出 JSON 或删除本地数据。

数据与支持

数据留在 iPhone，只有你主动使用 iOS 的“导出”或“分享”时才会交给你选择的目标位置。本版本没有账号、云同步、分析、广告、追踪、支付或后端服务。
