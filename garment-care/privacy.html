Privacy Policy

For Garment Care. Last updated: 2026-11-13.

This policy describes the Garment Care iPhone app. The app is an offline record organizer. The app does not collect, upload, or transmit your garment records, notes, photos, language preference, or local reminder data to the operator or a backend.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Garment names, areas, sides, dates, status history, care notes, and pressure-label text that you enter.
  • Photos that you choose with the system Photos picker. The app keeps a processed copy in its local app storage.
  • Your language choice and one-time reminders. Notifications are scheduled and delivered on-device by iOS.

No collection or third-party tracking

This build has no account, cloud sync, server storage, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, tracking, payment, HealthKit, or other backend service. The app does not automatically send records anywhere.

Export and Share

If you choose Export or Share, iOS lets you choose a destination such as Files or another app. The resulting copy is controlled by you and may be stored outside the app. The app cannot delete a copy that you shared.

Your controls

You can edit records, remove individual items, export a copy, or use Settings to delete all local data. Export before deleting the app or switching phones if you need to keep a copy. Uninstalling the app removes its local storage; exported or shared copies remain where you placed them.

Children

Garment Care is not directed to children under 13. The app has no account or data-collection flow.

Changes and contact

We will post changes to this page with a new last-updated date. Questions about this policy can be sent to vokie123@gmail.com.

Medical boundary: Garment Care only records what you enter. It does not diagnose, treat, select pressure, prescribe wear time, judge dryness or effectiveness, or control a medical device.

隐私政策

适用于压力衣轮换助手。最后更新：2026-11-13。

本政策适用于压力衣轮换助手 iPhone App。本 App 是离线记录工具，不会将你录入的衣物档案、备注、照片、语言偏好或本地提醒数据收集、上传或传输给运营方或后端服务。

保存在 iPhone 上的信息

  • 你录入的衣物名称、部位、左右侧、日期、状态历史、护理说明和压力标签原文。
  • 你通过系统照片选择器选择的照片。App 会将处理后的副本保存在本地。
  • 语言选择和一次性提醒。通知由 iOS 在设备端安排和发送。

没有收集和第三方追踪

本版本没有账号、云同步、服务端存储、分析、崩溃报告、广告、追踪、支付、HealthKit 或其他后端服务。App 不会自动把记录发送到任何地方。

导出和分享

如果你选择“导出”或“分享”，iOS 会让你选择“文件”或其他 App 等目标位置。生成的副本由你控制，可能保存在 App 之外；App 无法删除你已经分享的副本。

你的控制权

你可以编辑记录、删除单项、导出副本，或在“设置”中删除全部本地数据。卸载 App 或换手机前，如需保留记录请先导出。卸载会移除 App 的本地存储；已导出或分享的副本仍保留在你选择的位置。

儿童
