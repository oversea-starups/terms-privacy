Vocai Support

Vocai turns spoken or typed thoughts into organized notes and reviewable Apple Reminder drafts.

Get help

Email support@vocai.app with a short description of the issue, your iOS version, and your Vocai app version. Do not include passwords, authentication tokens, or sensitive note content.

Reminder access

Vocai asks for Reminders access only when you confirm that a draft should be saved. You can review and edit the title, date, time, and notes before confirming. Permission can be changed in iOS Settings.

Subscriptions

Purchases, renewals, cancellations, and refunds are managed by Apple. Open iOS Settings, tap your Apple Account, then Subscriptions to manage a Vocai subscription. Use Restore Purchases in Vocai when a valid purchase is not shown.
