Privacy Policy

Effective August 3, 2026

Vocai processes the information needed to create notes, prepare reminder drafts, operate accounts and subscriptions, and improve reliability. Vocai does not sell personal data and does not use third-party advertising or cross-company tracking.

1. Scope

This policy explains how Vocai ("we," "us," or the "App") handles information when you use the Vocai iOS application, its backend service, and related support pages.

2. Information we process

Account information

Sign in with Apple may provide an Apple account identifier, an email address, and a name, depending on what you choose to share. We use this information to create and secure your Vocai account. We do not receive your Apple password.

Notes and text content

Note titles, transcriptions, organized content, categories, tags, and related metadata are stored on your device. When you are signed in, this content is also sent to our backend so the App can save and synchronize your notes. Text submitted for AI organization is sent through our backend to the configured AI provider.

Audio and speech data

When you use voice features, the App accesses the microphone with permission. Live speech recognition uses Apple's Speech services and may be processed on-device or by Apple depending on device and system capabilities. When an audio transcription request is used, audio is uploaded to our backend and sent to the configured transcription provider. Our backend uses temporary processing files and removes them after the request completes. Your device may retain local recording data according to App and operating-system storage behavior.

Reminder requests

The reminder request you speak or type, together with the current time, timezone, and locale, is sent to our backend and configured AI provider to prepare an editable draft. The backend does not execute the reminder. After you review and explicitly confirm, the App uses Apple's EventKit framework to save one reminder to your selected Apple Reminders account. Vocai does not use reminder content for advertising and the current App does not read your existing reminder content.

Device, diagnostics, and usage information

We process a device identifier, device type, app version, session identifier, feature events, durations, result status, and error information. Analytics events are associated with the signed-in account and are used for security, quota enforcement, product measurement, and reliability. We do not include note text or audio in routine analytics event properties.

Purchase information

Apple processes payment details. Vocai receives product identifiers, transaction identifiers, subscription status, expiration information, and purchase verification data needed to unlock paid access and prevent fraud. We do not receive your full payment-card number.

3. How we use information

  • Authenticate accounts and maintain signed-in sessions.
  • Create, organize, store, synchronize, search, and display notes.
  • Prepare a reminder draft and save it only after confirmation.
  • Measure free usage, verify subscriptions, and restore purchases.
  • Detect abuse, diagnose failures, and improve performance.
  • Respond to support, legal, privacy, or security requests.

4. Service providers and disclosures

We disclose information only as needed to provide the feature you request, operate the service, comply with law, or protect users and the service.

  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, Speech, EventKit, StoreKit, operating-system permissions, and related platform services.
  • AI providers: OpenAI may process audio transcription requests. Depending on server configuration, OpenAI, Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (Bailian/Qwen), Zhipu AI, or Moonshot AI (Kimi) may process note or reminder text.
  • Infrastructure providers: Hosting, database, cache, networking, logging, and security vendors process data on our behalf to operate Vocai.

Provider processing is also governed by the provider's terms and privacy notices. We do not authorize providers to use Vocai content for advertising.

5. Storage and security

Vocai uses SwiftData for local app data and Keychain for authentication tokens. Server records are stored in access-controlled databases and caches. Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS where it travels over the network. No security method is perfect, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Account and synchronized note data are retained while your account is active or until you delete them, subject to limited backups, legal obligations, fraud prevention, and dispute resolution.
  • Backend audio processing files are temporary and removed after processing completes. AI providers may retain request data under their applicable service terms.
  • Usage and diagnostic records are retained as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, and improve the service, and are deleted with the account from the active Vocai database.
  • Apple retains purchase and subscription records under its own policies.
  • Reminders saved to Apple Reminders remain there until you delete them in Apple Reminders, even if you later delete Vocai or your Vocai account.

7. Your choices and rights

You can control microphone, speech recognition, and Reminders permissions in iOS Settings. You can edit or delete notes in the App, manage subscriptions through Apple, and delete your Vocai account from Settings in the App. Account deletion removes active server account data and local Vocai notes; it does not cancel an App Store subscription or delete reminders already saved in Apple Reminders.

Depending on your location, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive a copy of personal information. Contact us to make a request. We may need to verify your identity.

8. Sale, advertising, and tracking

Vocai does not sell personal information. Vocai does not display third-party advertising and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies for advertising or data-broker purposes.

9. Children

Vocai is not directed to children under 13, or the higher minimum age required in a user's jurisdiction. If we learn that we collected personal information from a child in violation of applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

10. International processing

Information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we use appropriate contractual or legal safeguards for international transfers.

11. Changes

We may update this policy as Vocai changes. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice when required by law.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email support@vocai.app.
