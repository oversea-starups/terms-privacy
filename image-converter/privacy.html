Privacy Policy

For Image Converter. Last updated: July 30 2026.

Image Converter Developer operates Image Converter. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, disclosed, and protected when you use the app, website, or related services.

Information We Collect

  • Privacy-safe app interaction events: selected image count; output format; quality preset; conversion outcome; export destination; paywall source; purchase outcome; review prompt request; and feedback category. Image content; filenames; file paths; feedback text; receipts; and email content are never sent to analytics.
  • Diagnostic data: crash domain and code; app version; iOS version; device model; and aggregate performance timing. Crash records are stripped of filenames; paths; and custom error text before upload.
  • Purchase status and product identifiers are processed by Apple StoreKit. The developer does not receive full payment card details.
  • Selected images and converted files are processed and stored only on the device. Firebase services may use network access; but image content is never uploaded.
  • Google Mobile Ads and UMP code is included for a possible future opt-in rewarded-ad feature. Production ad identifiers are not configured; so ads are not requested or shown in this release.
  • Purchase, subscription, or entitlement information processed through app stores or payment providers.
  • Analytics, diagnostics, performance, and crash information.

We collect information only as needed to provide, maintain, secure, and improve Image Converter.

How We Use Information

  • Provide and operate the service.
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot issues.
  • Respond to support, privacy, or legal requests.
  • Improve features, performance, and user experience.
  • Process purchases or subscriptions when applicable.
