  • Diagnose crashes, errors, abuse, and compatibility issues.
  • Manage advertising consent, respond to support, and comply with applicable law.

Third-Party Services

Advertising And Your Choices

Rewarded ads are optional, and the core game remains playable without watching ads. The app initializes AdMob only after UMP permits advertising requests. Declining personalized advertising or ATT tracking permission does not block core gameplay. Where required, you may reopen “Ad Privacy Options” inside the app, and you may change tracking permission in system settings.

We do not sell personal information. Google, Apple, and other service providers may process information only to provide the functions described above. We may disclose necessary information when required by law or to protect users and service security.

Your Choices And Rights
