Privacy Policy

For Material Smash. Last updated: 2026-07-23.

Material Smash Version 1.2 (build 4) is an offline iPhone game. The developer does not collect, store, or transmit personal data through this version of the app.

Version scope: The Version 1.2 statements on this page apply specifically to build 4. Version 1.1 may remain available during the App Store review and rollout period; the transition section below preserves the relevant earlier-version disclosures.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No account, login, contact information, identifiers, location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or attribution data.
  • No gameplay uploads or cloud storage. Version 1.2 offers no in-app purchase or subscription, and the developer receives no payment information.

Data Stored On Your Device

Material Smash saves gameplay progress, stage records, scores, in-game shards, discovered materials, tutorial state, and audio or haptic settings in Apple UserDefaults on your device. If you update from Version 1.1, the same local save may retain a legacy compatibility field named hasPurchasedRemoveAds, which records only whether Remove Ads was previously purchased. Version 1.2 does not use that field to show ads or offer paid features, and it is not sent to the developer. This local data is used only to restore or preserve your game. You can remove it by deleting the app; any device backups are controlled by Apple and your device settings.

Optional Screen Recording

The Creator Challenge can open Apple ReplayKit only after you choose Record Take. The microphone is disabled. Apple’s system preview lets you save, share, or discard the recording. The developer does not receive or upload the recording.

Apple Services

The app is distributed through the Apple App Store and can use Apple ReplayKit at your request. Those Apple services are governed by Apple’s terms and privacy policy. Version 1.2 (build 4) does not initialize or enable Game Center, in-app purchases, advertising, analytics, or third-party SDKs.

Version 1.1 Transition

The legal disclosures previously published for Version 1.1 described Game Center, a Remove Ads purchase, and possible advertising. Those earlier disclosures continue to describe Version 1.1 for users who still have that version installed during the App Store transition. They do not describe Version 1.2 (build 4), where those flows are not enabled or presented. You can check the installed app version in Material Smash Settings.

This Legal Website

This website stores only your English or Chinese language preference in browser local storage. It uses no analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.

Children

Material Smash is not directed to children under 13, and the app does not collect personal data. If you have a privacy concern involving a child, contact us.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new last-updated date.

隐私政策

适用于 Material Smash。最后更新：2026-07-23。

Material Smash 1.2 版本（构建 4）是一款可离线运行的 iPhone 游戏。开发者不会通过此版本的应用收集、存储或传输个人数据。

版本范围：本页面关于 1.2 版本的说明仅适用于构建 4。在 App Store 审核和分阶段发布期间，1.1 版本可能仍然可用；下方的版本过渡章节保留了与旧版本有关的披露。

我们不收集的数据

  • 不收集账号、登录信息、联系方式、设备标识、位置、通讯录、照片、相机或麦克风数据。
  • 不收集分析、崩溃报告、广告或归因数据。
  • 不上传游戏数据，也不使用云存储。1.2 版本不提供 App 内购买或订阅，开发者也不会收到支付信息。

存储在设备上的数据

Material Smash 使用 Apple UserDefaults 在你的设备上保存游戏进度、关卡记录、分数、游戏内碎片、已发现材质、教学状态，以及声音和触感设置。如果你从 1.1 版本升级，同一份本地存档可能保留名为 hasPurchasedRemoveAds 的旧版兼容字段；它只记录你此前是否购买过“移除广告”。1.2 版本不会用该字段展示广告或提供付费功能，也不会把它发送给开发者。这些本地数据仅用于恢复或兼容你的游戏。你可以通过删除应用移除这些数据；设备备份由 Apple 和你的设备设置控制。

可选的屏幕录制

只有当你在创作者挑战中主动选择“录制本局”时，应用才会打开 Apple ReplayKit。麦克风保持关闭。你可以在 Apple 系统预览中保存、分享或丢弃录像；开发者不会接收或上传录像。

Apple 服务

本应用通过 Apple App Store 分发，并可在你主动请求时使用 Apple ReplayKit。相关 Apple 服务受 Apple 的条款和隐私政策约束。1.2 版本（构建 4）不会初始化或启用 Game Center、App 内购买、广告、分析或第三方 SDK。

1.1 版本过渡

此前为 1.1 版本发布的法律披露说明了 Game Center、“移除广告”购买以及可能的广告配置。在 App Store 版本过渡期间，仍安装并使用 1.1 版本的用户继续适用这些旧版披露。它们不描述 1.2 版本（构建 4）；后者不会启用或展示上述流程。你可以在 Material Smash 的“设置”中查看当前安装版本。

本法律网站

本网站仅在浏览器本地存储中保存你的中英文语言偏好，不使用分析、广告或跟踪 Cookie。

儿童

Material Smash 并非面向 13 岁以下儿童，且应用不会收集个人数据。如你对涉及儿童的隐私问题有疑虑，请联系我们。

变更
