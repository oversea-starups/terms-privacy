Support

Material Smash works offline and does not require an account. For questions, bug reports, or feedback, email vokie123@gmail.com. Including your iPhone model, iOS version, and app version helps us investigate technical issues. Never send passwords or payment information.

  • Sound and haptics can be changed from Settings inside the app.
  • Creator Take recording is optional and uses Apple ReplayKit on supported physical devices.
  • Progress is stored locally on the device. Deleting the app may remove local progress.
  • An update from Version 1.1 may retain a legacy local Remove Ads status solely for save compatibility; Version 1.2 does not use it to enable ads or commerce.

用户支持

Material Smash 可以离线运行，也不需要账号。如有问题、错误报告或建议，请发送邮件至 vokie123@gmail.com。如需排查技术问题，建议注明 iPhone 型号、iOS 版本和 App 版本。请勿发送密码或支付信息。

  • 可在 App 内的设置页面调整声音和触感反馈。
  • 创作者挑战录制为可选功能，并在支持的实体设备上使用 Apple ReplayKit。
  • 游戏进度保存在设备本地；删除 App 可能会移除本地进度。
  • 从 1.1 版本升级时，可能仅为存档兼容而保留旧版“移除广告”本地状态；1.2 版本不会用它启用广告或商业功能。
