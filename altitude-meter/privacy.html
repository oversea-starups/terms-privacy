Privacy Policy

For Altitude Meter / 海拔测量仪. Last updated: 2026-07-31.

Oversea Startups operates Altitude Meter / 海拔测量仪. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, disclosed, and protected when you use the app, website, or related services.

Information We Collect

  • Location: with permission the app reads precise or approximate location including altitude; coordinates; horizontal and vertical accuracy; heading; and timestamps. These values support live measurements; map positioning; optional address lookup; and records you choose to create. The app does not create a location-history account.
  • Motion and pressure: with permission and supported hardware the app reads barometer data to display current pressure and relative altitude change. This is not health or medical data.
  • Photos and camera: photos you take or select are processed on-device to render a watermark. The app does not upload photos to a developer server. A result leaves the app only when you save it or choose a destination in the iOS share sheet.
  • Purchases and preferences: Apple processes payments. The app stores unlock state; map choice; altitude unit; and keep-awake preference in private on-device settings.
  • Map and place name: when you enable Map & Address, Apple MapKit displays your current area and Apple CLGeocoder looks up a place name. The app does not include a third-party map SDK or developer-operated map server.
  • No account; advertising; first-party behavioral analytics; developer cloud sync; or AI feature is provided.
  • Purchase and entitlement information processed by Apple for the one-time Permanent Premium purchase.

The app has no developer account system and does not upload measurement history to a developer server.

How We Use Information

  • Display current altitude, accuracy, coordinates, pressure, relative altitude, and compass direction on your device.
  • Render a map and request address text only when you enable map services.
  • Create altitude cards and photo watermarks after your action.
  • Verify or restore the one-time Permanent Premium purchase through Apple.
  • Respond to email support, privacy, or legal requests that you send to us.

Third-Party Services

Measurement, the optional map interface, place-name lookup, photos, sharing, and purchases use Apple system frameworks and services. The app does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or third-party map SDKs.

  • Apple Core Location and Core Motion
  • Apple MapKit and CLGeocoder
  • Apple Photos, Camera, and sharing services
  • Apple App Store and StoreKit

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information, use app data for cross-app tracking, or transmit your readings or photos to a developer-operated server. Apple may process information required to provide system maps, place-name lookup, permissions, sharing, and purchases under Apple's policies. We may disclose information if required by law.

Data Retention

Current measurements and selected photos are not stored on a developer server. App preferences and purchase unlock state remain on your device until you change them or delete the app. Apple applies its own retention practices to information processed by Apple services.

Your Choices And Rights

You may use Measurement Only without the map or place-name interface, change that preference in Settings, change iOS permissions, and delete local app data by deleting the app. Contact us for privacy assistance. Some rights depend on your location and applicable law.

Children

Altitude Meter / 海拔测量仪 is not directed to children under 13 unless explicitly stated in the service. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

International Users

Apple may process information in countries or regions other than where you live according to its policies and applicable law.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new last-updated date.

隐私政策

适用于 Altitude Meter / 海拔测量仪。最后更新：2026-07-31。

Oversea Startups 运营 Altitude Meter / 海拔测量仪。本隐私政策说明你在使用应用、网站或相关服务时，我们如何收集、使用、披露和保护信息。

我们收集的信息

  • 位置信息：经你授权后，App 读取精确或大致位置，包括海拔；经纬度；水平与垂直精度；方向和时间戳。这些值用于实时测量；地图定位；可选地址查询以及你主动创建的记录。App 不创建位置历史账号。
  • 运动与气压：经你授权且设备支持时，App 读取气压计数据以显示当前气压和相对高度变化，不作为健康或医疗数据。
  • 照片与相机：你拍摄或选择的照片仅在设备本地处理并生成水印，不上传至开发者服务器。只有你保存或在 iOS 分享面板中选择目标后，成品才会离开 App。
  • 购买与偏好：付款由 Apple 处理；App 仅在设备私有设置中保存解锁状态；地图选择；高度单位和屏幕常亮偏好。
  • 地图与地点名称：当你开启“地图与地址”时，Apple MapKit 显示当前区域，Apple CLGeocoder 查询地点名称。App 不包含第三方地图 SDK，也不使用开发者运营的地图服务器。
  • App 不提供账号；广告；自有行为统计；开发者云同步或 AI 功能。
  • 由 Apple 为一次性“永久高级版”购买处理的购买与权益信息。

App 不设开发者账号系统，也不会把测量历史上传至开发者服务器。

我们如何使用信息

  • 在你的设备上显示当前海拔、精度、坐标、气压、相对高度与指南针方向。
  • 仅在你启用地图服务后渲染地图并请求地址文字。
  • 在你主动操作后生成海拔卡片与照片水印。
  • 通过 Apple 验证或恢复一次性的永久高级版购买。
  • 回复你主动发送的支持、隐私或法律邮件。
