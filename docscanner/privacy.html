Privacy Policy

For DocScanner. Last updated: 2026-07-29.

Beijing baqijiuyi Technology Co., Ltd operates DocScanner. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

Information Handling

DocScanner does not collect personal information or transmit document content to the developer. Scanned images, recognized text, document titles, categories, favorites, and PDFs are stored only in the app container on your device.

How We Use Information

  • On-device text recognition makes scans searchable and editable.
  • Local metadata supports titles, categories, favorites, and page order.
  • Files leave the app only when you choose a system share destination.
  • Face ID or device authentication is requested only if you enable Privacy Lock.

Third-Party Services

DocScanner contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, cloud storage, account, or third-party SDK integrations. Apple provides app distribution, document capture, text recognition, device authentication, photo selection, and system sharing under Apple's terms and privacy practices.

Data Sharing

We do not receive, sell, rent, or share your document content. When you use the system share sheet, you choose the destination and that destination's privacy practices apply.

Data Retention

Documents remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. Device backups are controlled by your Apple and device settings.

Your Choices And Rights

You can rename, edit, share, or delete documents inside the app. Because the developer does not receive your document content, requests to access or delete that local content must be completed on your device.

Children

DocScanner is not directed to children under 13 unless explicitly stated in the service. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new last-updated date.

隐私政策

适用于 DocScanner。最后更新：2026-07-29。

Beijing baqijiuyi Technology Co., Ltd 运营 DocScanner。本隐私政策说明你在使用 App 时，App 如何处理信息。

信息处理

DocScanner 不收集个人信息，也不会向开发者传输文档内容。扫描图像、识别文本、文档标题、分类、收藏状态和 PDF 仅存储在你设备上的 App 容器中。

我们如何使用信息

  • 在设备上识别文本，使扫描内容可搜索和编辑。
  • 使用本地元数据保存标题、分类、收藏状态和页面顺序。
  • 仅当你主动选择系统分享目标时，文件才会离开 App。
  • 仅在你启用隐私锁时请求 Face ID 或设备认证。

第三方服务

DocScanner 不含广告、分析、跟踪、云存储、账号或第三方 SDK 集成。Apple 根据其条款和隐私实践提供 App 分发、文档扫描、文本识别、设备认证、照片选择和系统分享能力。

数据共享

我们不会接收、出售、出租或共享你的文档内容。使用系统分享表单时，分享目标由你选择，并适用该目标自身的隐私实践。

数据保留

文档会保留在你的设备上，直到你删除文档或卸载 App。设备备份由你的 Apple 和设备设置控制。

你的选择和权利

你可以在 App 内重命名、编辑、分享或删除文档。由于开发者不会接收文档内容，访问或删除这些本地内容需在你的设备上完成。

儿童

除非服务中另有明确说明，DocScanner 并非面向 13 岁以下儿童。如果你认为儿童向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们以便采取适当措施。

变更

我们可能不时更新本隐私政策。更新后的版本会发布在本页面，并标注新的最后更新日期。
