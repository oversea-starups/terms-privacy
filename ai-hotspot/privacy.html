Privacy Policy

For AI Pulse: AI News & Perks. Last updated: November 24, 2026.

Summary: AI Pulse does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Everything you save stays on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Pulse ("the app", "we") handles information when you use the app. The app is a reader for publicly available AI news and a directory of publicly advertised free tiers, credits, and discounts ("perks").

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-in is required, and we do not collect names, email addresses, or contact details.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage telemetry is sent to us or to any third party.
  • No advertising identifiers are used, and the app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data is accessed.

Data Stored On Your Device

Items you bookmark are saved locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData). This data never leaves your device, is not uploaded to any server, and is removed when you delete the app. You can delete any saved item at any time within the app.

Network Requests

To display content, the app downloads two publicly available JSON files (a news feed and a perks list) hosted on GitHub Pages. These requests are standard HTTPS GET requests for public content. We do not operate a backend that receives personal data, and no user identifier is attached to these requests. The app also bundles a copy of the content so it works offline.

External Links

News items and perks link to third-party websites (for example, model providers and publications). When you open a link, it loads in an in-app Safari view controlled by Apple's Safari services. Those third-party sites have their own privacy policies, and any data you provide to them is governed by their policies, not ours. We do not control and are not responsible for third-party content or practices.

Children

The app provides unrestricted access to the open web through external links and is rated 17+. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information, because we do not collect any.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new last-updated date.

隐私政策

适用于 AI Pulse（AI 热点与福利）。最后更新：2026 年 11 月 24 日。

摘要：AI Pulse 不收集、不存储、不共享任何个人数据。没有账号、没有分析统计、没有广告、没有追踪。你收藏的所有内容都只保存在你的设备上。

本隐私政策说明你使用 AI Pulse（下称「本应用」「我们」）时，我们如何处理信息。本应用是一个公开 AI 新闻阅读器，以及公开宣传的免费额度、积分与折扣（下称「福利」）目录。

我们收集的信息

我们不收集任何个人信息。具体而言：

  • 无需账号或登录，我们不收集姓名、邮箱或联系方式。
  • 不会向我们或任何第三方发送分析、崩溃报告或使用遥测数据。
  • 不使用广告标识符，也不请求 App Tracking Transparency 权限。
  • 不访问位置、通讯录、照片、麦克风或相机数据。

存储在你设备上的数据

你收藏的内容通过 Apple 的本地存储（SwiftData）保存在你的设备上。这些数据绝不会离开你的设备，不会上传到任何服务器，删除应用时即被清除。你可以随时在应用内删除任何已收藏内容。

网络请求

为展示内容，本应用会下载两个托管在 GitHub Pages 上的公开 JSON 文件（新闻源与福利列表）。这些请求是获取公开内容的标准 HTTPS GET 请求。我们不运营接收个人数据的后端，请求中也不附带任何用户标识。应用还内置了一份内容副本，以便离线使用。

外部链接

新闻与福利会链接到第三方网站（例如模型提供方与媒体）。当你打开链接时，会在由 Apple Safari 服务控制的应用内 Safari 视图中加载。这些第三方网站有各自的隐私政策，你向其提供的任何数据受其政策约束，而非本政策。我们无法控制也不对第三方内容或行为负责。

儿童

本应用通过外部链接提供对开放网络的无限制访问，分级为 17+。本应用并非面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不会故意收集儿童信息。

数据共享与出售

我们不会出售、出租或共享个人信息，因为我们根本不收集任何个人信息。

变更
