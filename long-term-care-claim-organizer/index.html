Long-Term Care Claim Organizer Support

Last updated: 2026-07-30

Get help with the local-first iPhone app for organizing long-term-care claim facts and documents.

Independent organizer: The app is not an insurer portal. It does not submit claims, interpret policies, determine coverage, confirm receipt, predict payment, or provide legal, financial, insurance, or medical advice.

Quick help

  • Create a claim workspace, then record policy wording, elimination-period facts, care logs, materials, calls, invoices, and reminders.
  • Import supported receipts and documents only when you choose them through Apple's Files interface.
  • Use the monthly record check to find source records that may need attention. It is not an insurer checklist or eligibility decision.
  • Export selected facts as PDF, CSV, or JSON. Original attachment files are shared separately and only when you choose.
  • Delete one claim from its workspace, or delete all app-controlled data from Settings.

Troubleshooting

If protected storage is unavailable, unlock and restart the iPhone, then reopen the app. If a file will not import, confirm it is a supported regular file under 50 MB and is downloaded in Files. Notification delivery also depends on iOS permission, Focus, and device settings.
