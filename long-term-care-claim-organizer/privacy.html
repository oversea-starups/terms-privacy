Privacy Policy

For Long-Term Care Claim Organizer. Effective and last updated: 2026-07-30.

Beijing baqijiuyi Technology Co., Ltd operates Long-Term Care Claim Organizer. The app is independent productivity software for adults organizing facts and documents related to a long-term-care insurance claim.

Version 1.0 has no account, advertising, tracking, analytics, in-app purchase, cloud sync, or developer-operated claim-content server. Claim content is processed on the iPhone and is not collected by the developer.

Information you place in the app

You may enter or import claim, claimant, insurer, policy, elimination-period, care, ADL, provider, service, amount, invoice, material, call, reference, reminder, receipt, and attachment information. This may include personal, health-related, identity, contact, or financial information about you or others. Enter it only when you have authority to do so.

On-device storage and processing

Claim records and imported files are stored in the app's protected container on your iPhone. Core features work offline. The app does not send claim content to Beijing baqijiuyi Technology Co., Ltd. App data is excluded from automatic backup and there is no developer recovery copy. Device loss, uninstalling the app, or deleting data may therefore be irreversible.

Apple system features

  • Files: the app accesses only files you select through Apple's document picker and copies supported files into app-owned storage.
  • Notifications: optional reminders use local notifications with generic lock-screen text. Apple controls permission and delivery.
  • Preview, export, and share: a destination you explicitly choose may receive a copy under its own privacy terms. The app never selects a carrier or recipient for you.
  • App Store and iOS: Apple may process store, device, and system information under Apple's policies.

Exports and retention

You choose the claim scope and format before generating PDF, CSV, or JSON files. Fact exports include attachment index metadata but not original attachment bytes. App-controlled content remains until you delete a record, claim, all app data, or the app. Copies saved elsewhere remain under the destination's control and cannot be recalled by deleting the app.

Your controls

You can review, edit, export, and delete user-entered records; delete attachments; delete a claim; revoke notification permission; or use Settings → Export or delete data → Delete all app data. Because the developer does not receive on-device claim content, support cannot retrieve, correct, export, delete, or recover it for you.

Support communications

If you email vokie123@gmail.com, we receive the address, message, and technical details you choose to provide. We use them to answer the request, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations, and retain them only as reasonably necessary for those purposes. Do not send claim or health content. We do not sell support information or use it for targeted advertising.

Children and international users

The app is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. Privacy rights concerning information the developer actually receives, such as support email, vary by region. Contact us to exercise an applicable right. These rights do not give the developer technical access to content stored only on your iPhone.

Security and changes

iOS Data Protection and app-container controls reduce risk but cannot guarantee security on an unlocked, shared, jailbroken, or compromised device. Use a device passcode and review sharing and lock-screen settings. We may update this policy when the app or applicable requirements change and will post the new date here.
