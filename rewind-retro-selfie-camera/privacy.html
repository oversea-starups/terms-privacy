Third-Party Services

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share photos or personal information because the app does not send that data to us. Apple may process purchase and account information separately when you use an in-app purchase.

Data Retention

We have no developer-server copy of your photos or face-position data to retain. You can delete saved photos in the Photos app and remove local app settings by deleting the app. If you email us, we may retain the message as reasonably needed to answer and document the request.

Your Choices And Rights

You control camera and photo-library permissions in iOS Settings. You may contact us for privacy assistance. Purchase history and subscription controls are managed by Apple.
