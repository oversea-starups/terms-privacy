Version 1.0 has no account or login, purchases or subscriptions, advertising or attribution SDK, analytics or crash-reporting SDK, cloud sync, AI feature, or developer-operated backend.

Third-Party Services

Apple distributes the app through the App Store and may process information under Apple's own privacy policy. Unscrew Calm does not send gameplay or personal data to the developer or to a third-party service.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information because the app does not collect it.

Data Retention

The app's gameplay data remains on your device until you reset the device data or remove the app. The developer does not retain a copy.

Your Choices And Rights

You can remove locally stored app data by deleting the app. For privacy or support questions, contact us at the email below.

Children

Unscrew Calm is not directed to children under 13 unless explicitly stated in the service. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

International Users

The app does not transmit personal information to the developer for international processing.
