We do not sell or share personal data collected through the app because the app does not transmit personal data to us. Support email information may be disclosed only when required by law or necessary to protect rights and safety.

On-device settings remain on your device until you change them, reset app data, or delete the app. Support emails are kept only as long as reasonably needed to answer the request, maintain security, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

You can remove on-device settings by deleting the app. You may contact us about a support email or exercise privacy rights available in your location.
