Privacy policy

ShotGrove makes your pieces on your iPhone. You can use the app without an account.

Updated November 18, 2026

Your pictures, clips and projects

The system photo picker passes the items you choose to ShotGrove. Local copies are used to create and reopen projects. Captions, drafts, exported pieces and favorites stay on the device. Creation does not require access to your camera, microphone or contacts.

Permissions and sharing

Saving to Photos asks for permission to add pictures and videos. You can change that permission in iOS Settings. Sharing opens the system share sheet, which sends the file to the destination you choose.

Reference links and other services

Links you paste or share are saved locally. The share extension passes references to the app through an on-device shared container. An outside platform handles visits to its pages under its own policy.

Collection and retention

This version has no accounts, advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. ShotGrove does not receive or store your pictures, clips, captions, projects or favorites on a server. They remain on your device until you delete them. iOS handles device backups according to your settings.

Deleting data and changing permissions

Delete one piece in 项目, or clear the app’s data in 我的 → 清空本地数据. Manage copies saved in Photos, Files or another service at that destination. You can change Photos access in iOS Settings.

Visiting this website

These pages use locally hosted images and styles, with no site analytics scripts, advertisements or upload forms. The host receives network request information needed to serve the pages. The website cannot read pictures or projects from your app.

When you email us

The recipient and email providers receive your sending address, message and any attachments you include. We use this information to respond to your support or privacy request. You can ask about or request deletion of an earlier message at the address below.

Contact the developer

You can email us about a problem or privacy request. Tell us which game you were using and what happened.
